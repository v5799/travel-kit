Luggage forwarding steps (first-timer):
1. Ask the hotel front desk (FLAG) for a Yamato "Takkyubin" form (they help in English).
2. Write recipient: THE KNOT TOKYO Shinjuku, name of guest, check-in date, hotel address (no phone numbers needed here; the desk handles).
3. Choose size: Kanto-Kansai table July 2026: size 140 ~2,310; size 160 ~2,630 (verify).
4. Hand over by the pickup cut-off the evening before (Oct 18). Normally arrives next day; some reports >24 h. Aim: send Oct 18 -> arrives Oct 19-20; check-in is 15:00 Oct 19.
5. Carry a day bag (clothes for 1 night) on the train; keep passport/valuables with you.
6. Ask THE KNOT to confirm they accept parcels before arrival (verify). Owner of this task: OPEN. Varun stays in a different Osaka hotel and sends his own bag.
